How to start

In Termux, run termux-setup-storage once and grant permission. Then run moviebox-tui --web and open http://127.0.0.1:8787 in your phone browser.

Background operation

Android may stop Termux to save battery. Exclude Termux from battery optimization for long downloads. This browser page cannot keep the service alive by itself.

Source limitations

This first milestone downloads direct-file sources only. Resolution, audio variants and subtitles depend on the source. Segmented streams are marked unsupported instead of being saved as invalid video files.

Privacy

The service binds to 127.0.0.1 only. It is not exposed to other devices on your Wi-Fi network.
